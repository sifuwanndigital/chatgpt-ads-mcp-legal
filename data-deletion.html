User Data Deletion

ChatGPT Ads MCP · Sifuwann – Klien PNS

To remove the app’s access and request deletion of information associated with your Meta connection:

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites or Business integrations.
  3. Select ChatGPT Ads MCP and choose Remove.
  4. If Facebook offers an option to delete information shared with the app, select it and confirm.
  5. In ChatGPT, open the plugin’s settings and disconnect the connected Meta account.

Removing the integration revokes future access. Any app-controlled information associated with the connection will be deleted or de-identified within 30 days unless retention is required by law or for security purposes. Data held independently by Meta or ChatGPT is governed by those providers’ policies and account controls.
