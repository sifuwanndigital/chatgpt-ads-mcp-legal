Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ChatGPT Ads MCP, operated by Sifuwann – Klien PNS (“we”, “us”, or “our”), accesses and uses information when an authorized user connects a Meta account.

Information we access

Depending on the permissions approved by the user, the integration may access Meta account identifiers, business portfolios, Pages, advertising accounts, campaigns, ad sets, advertisements, creatives, catalog information, lead-related information, Instagram business information, and advertising performance metrics.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide user-requested advertising workflows, including account discovery, campaign planning and management, reporting, performance analysis, and optimization. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by Meta and by the ChatGPT service used to operate the integration. We disclose information only when required to provide the requested service, comply with law, protect rights and security, or with the user’s direction.

Storage and security

We limit access to authorized users and apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service or meet legal obligations. OAuth access can be revoked by the user at any time.

User choices and deletion

Users may revoke the app’s Meta permissions through Facebook settings and may request deletion by following our User Data Deletion instructions.

Changes

We may update this policy when the integration or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
